About Me

Hello! I am born in Qingyang (青阳), a small town in Anhui (安徽), China. In 2025, I obtained my PhD in Physics from the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST), under the supervision of Prof. Adrian Po. Currently, I am a postdoctoral researcher at the National University of Singapore working with Prof. Liujun Zou.

My research focuses on theoretical problems in quantum many-body systems, employing both analytical techniques and numerical methods. I also have a strong interest in mathematical physics and quantum information theory.

Research Interests

Quantum Many-Body Systems

Study of emergent phenomena in complex quantum systems with large numbers of interacting particles.

Tensor Networks

Development and application of tensor network methods for simulating quantum systems.

Quantum Information

Exploration of information theoretical perspective in quantum systems, including the estimation of entanglement measures, the preparation and detection of quantum states etc.

Topological Phases

Investigation of topological order and anyonic excitations in quantum matter.
