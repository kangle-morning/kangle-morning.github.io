Research Focus

My research lies at the intersection of condensed matter physics, quantum information theory, and computational physics. I develop and apply novel theoretical frameworks to understand complex quantum systems, with particular emphasis on:

  • Topological phases of matter and their classification
  • Tensor network methods for quantum simulation
  • Quantum entanglement in many-body systems
  • Non-equilibrium dynamics of quantum systems
  • Quantum algorithms for condensed matter problems
